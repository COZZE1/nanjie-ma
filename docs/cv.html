Selected comments

Short excerpts from remarks kept in the studio resume. Full texts stay in the archive.

  • Fu Sen (curator): The artist proposes a very ambitious framework that treats art as an intellectual practice — a “quantum narrative” that refuses a single fixed meaning and looks for meaning in uncertainty and collapse. Even while the theory is still at a formative stage, that ambition is philosophically and aesthetically enlightening in itself.
  • Xiao Jian (World Documentary Photographers Association): Works that use science to deconstruct art and then rediscover it through aesthetic verification — colour formulas, dynamic parameters, and spatial metaphors. The hedge between warm reds/yellows and cold blues/greens (smoke energy against cooler calm) builds a tension like ice and fire that matches the contradictions and balances in the quantum narrative.
