Quantum Narrative Matrix

White Tiger

Painting · canvas

A white tiger walks the vertical field; red ground and cooler patches hold handwritten studio marks. The resume places this work with the studio’s art–science practice notes: attention to spatial depth and colour purity so the traditional motif reads clearly against the notation.

Those marks include operational studio formulas meant to be worked with the picture. Longer perception notes and theoretical writing belong on the separate research door when published there.

Adapted from the studio resume practice note on White Tiger. Shown in the Quantum Narrative Matrix series.
