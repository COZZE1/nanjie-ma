Quantum Narrative Matrix

Quantum Genesis

Painting · canvas · 2025

In 2025, quantum technology and artificial intelligence are accelerating. Quantum Genesis belongs with the studio practice under the name Quantum Narrative School. The hot cloud mass carries a sense of outburst — the violence of technical speed in this decade. The two monkey-like, nearly human figures stand for carbon-based life. Faced with a silicon-based intelligence boom, those bodies look small, like monkeys — a metaphor for constrained “free will,” as if coded and preset, not a laboratory forecast.

Operators and silver paths on the surface are original studio formulas in three kinds: operational (meant to be calculated or worked through with the picture), adjustment-in-progress (still being tuned), and metaphorical (analogy on the surface). Some marks here are operational — they can be run as studio calculations alongside the image, not as ornament. Longer theoretical writing lives on a separate site.

I take this to be art that could only appear in this era — as if, for a moment, we had glimpsed the Creator’s code. Here “Creator” is the studio metaphor for the order that builds beings and relations, not a theological claim.

Adapted from the studio work note for Quantum Genesis.
