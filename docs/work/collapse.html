Quantum Narrative Matrix

Collapse

Painting · canvas

The title borrows everyday physics language — a body shrinking under force — and the familiar quantum-mechanics image of a wave function settling into one outcome. The studio text also speaks of “contextual collapse” as a working metaphor inside the series.

Formulas on the canvas fall into three kinds: operational (meant to be calculated or worked through with the picture), adjustment-in-progress, and metaphorical. Operational marks are runnable with the image. Longer theoretical writing lives on a separate site. Judge the painting first.

Adapted from the studio resume entry for Collapse.
