About

Painter and photographer.

I make paintings and photographs. I work in China now, after training in Venezia, and I plan to work abroad again when the right studio or residency opens. Quantum Narrative School is the studio and series name: a working umbrella for pictures and related projects, not a second diploma.

I trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia and remain grateful for that formation — painting under Prof. Igor Lecic; sculpture under Prof. Giuseppe La Bruna; also studying with Saverio Simi de Burgis. The Academy’s habit of looking, making, and arguing in the studio still shapes the work.

I support liberal democracy. Without it, I do not believe humanity has a future. I hold no political office. The paintings are not a party platform.

I am a member of the Shaanxi Artists Association. In April 2025 I presented a solo exhibition in Xi’an (see Exhibition). China is part of the factual record of the practice — membership, shows, and press — alongside the Venetian years that formed the studio habit. Judge the pictures on the wall; dates and scans sit on the CV.

Some canvases carry formulas, diagrams, and invented notation. In the studio list they fall into three kinds: operational (to be calculated or worked through), adjustment-in-progress, and metaphorical (analogy). Operational marks are meant to run as studio calculations with the picture — not decoration. Longer theoretical writing has its own door: q-n-s.com.

For residencies I look for time in a painting studio, with room for a camera, in Europe or Asia.
