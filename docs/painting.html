English titles follow the studio resume list. Marks on the canvas include original formulas the studio divides into three kinds: operational (meant to be calculated or worked through), adjustment-in-progress (still being tuned), and metaphorical (analogy on the surface). Operational marks are runnable studio calculations with the image — not ornament. Longer theoretical writing lives on a separate site.
