## Visa (not legal advice)

Italy digital nomad / remote-worker entry is an **Italian** consular procedure, not something the Chinese Embassy in Italy issues.

Planning note from chat used “about €25k+/year”. Consular checklists in circulation use a **higher** highly-qualified threshold (often cited around **€28,000**, and some Italian pages point at ISTAT-linked figures near **€33,500**). Bank statements must be checked against the **PDF on the consulate that will actually take the file**. See `applications/digital_nomad_italy_checklist_EN.md`.
