## Mix rule

One IG account may carry painting, photography, and research posts. Bio:

`Nanjie Ma — Painting · Photography · Cosmological narrative`

Tags: `#painting` `#photography` `#studio` plus the series title in English when it exists.

Do not post covering-kernel mill tables as captions. Research posts should still look like a person thinking in public, then point to https://www.q-n-s.com/ when a paper is the object.

## Money from views

YouTube Partner (ads) is the only “play = pay” path that is worth planning. IG / Facebook Reels play is not a wage. X Original Content Rewards need Premium and high authenticated reach.

Do not build a YouTube channel only for ads before 1000 subscribers and the hour / Shorts thresholds.
