# Artist statement

Nanjie Ma  
Painter and photographer  
Quantum Narrative School (studio name)  
Based in China · trained in Venezia

I work in painting and photography. The pictures are the work: surface, colour, figure, and whatever marks the studio puts on the canvas. Original formulas on the work fall into three studio categories — operational (meant to be calculated or worked through), adjustment-in-progress, and metaphorical. Operational marks are runnable with the image; they are not ornament. Metaphorical marks stay analogy on the surface.

Quantum Narrative School is the studio and series name for that practice. It is not a second diploma. Longer theoretical writing, when published, has its own site.

I trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia and exhibit as a painter. I work in China now and look for residencies abroad that can hold both studio painting and a camera, without forcing the work into illustration or concept-art production alone.

Selected titles from the studio list: *Quantum Genesis*, *Collapse*, *White Tiger*, the series *Cognitive Dynamics*, and *From the Appearance to the Disappearance of an Idea*.
