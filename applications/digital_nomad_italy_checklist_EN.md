# Italy digital nomad / remote worker — holder checklist

**Not legal advice.** The file that counts is the checklist PDF on the **Italian embassy or consulate that will take your appointment**. Figures and document lists change.

## Which office does what

| Office | Role |
|--------|------|
| Italian Embassy / Consulate in your **country of legal residence** | National visa for digital nomad / remote worker. This is the application. |
| Example consular PDF (Shanghai, self-employment digital nomad, 2025 rev., hosted 2026-08) | https://consshanghai.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Checklist-NOMADE-DIGITALE_2025.rev_.pdf |
| Italian government explainer | https://integrazionemigranti.gov.it/en-gb/Ricerca-news/Dettaglio-news/id/3835/t/30/Who-are-the-digital-nomads-How-can-they-enter-Italy- |
| Chinese Embassy / Consulate **in Italy** | PRC passport and consular protection **after** you are in Italy. They do **not** issue the Italian nomad visa. |

If you are already living in Italy on another status, do not assume you can convert in-country. Many posts require applying from the consulate of residence.

## Planning numbers vs official numbers

A planning chat used “about €25k+/year”. **Do not file on that memory.**

Circulating guides in 2026 often cite about **€28,000** per year from non-Italian sources, and some Italian pages point at an ISTAT-linked highly-qualified floor near **€33,500**. Dependants add more. Use the number printed on **your** consulate PDF.

- [ ] Criminal-record certificates as listed, apostille / translation if required
- [ ] After entry: permesso di soggiorno window (Italian pages commonly say within 8 days)
